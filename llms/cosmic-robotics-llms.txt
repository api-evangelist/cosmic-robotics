# Cosmic Robotics

> Cosmic Robotics builds autonomous construction equipment to build critical
> infrastructure. The first frontier: solar.

Cosmic Robotics, Inc. designs and operates autonomous machines that take on the hardest,
most dangerous jobs on a construction site, coordinated as a fleet by a single software
layer. The largest infrastructure buildout in history needs new equipment; Cosmic builds it.
First market: utility-scale solar energy, expanding to data center construction.

## What we build

- **Cosmic-1A**: a new class of autonomous construction equipment — robotic solar panel
  installation for utility-scale energy. A unified, purpose-built platform for the toughest jobs on site.
- **Constellation**: the coordination and control layer for jobsite autonomy — one system to
  plan, dispatch, and monitor the fleet. https://constellation.cosmicrobotics.com
